How it works

Three Bayesian models, each validated on held-out seasons: penalty-shot success (shooter and goalie effects, from every PWHL shootout attempt with an NHL-informed prior), 4-on-3 power-play scoring (PWHL 5-on-4 rate times a multiplier learned from eleven NHL seasons), and overtime win probability (a 3-on-3 scoring rate that rises through overtime, plus the shootout). For each of the model's posterior draws the calculator compares the two options; the percentage is the share of draws in which the penalty shot wins more often.

Limitations: few PWHL overtime penalties exist, so power-play scoring leans on NHL data; shooter differences are small because shootout success is mostly luck; players with no PWHL shootout history count as league average; the shootout uses each team's top five shooters from the 2026-27 rosters against the goalies selected above.
